### 💫 About Me

I am a PhD student in **Transportation Engineering at the University of Mississippi (Ole Miss)**, working as a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant under **Dr. Amir M. Molan**. My research focuses on the **safety and operational performance of Alternative Intersections and Interchanges (AII)**, including RCUTs, MUTs, DDIs and roundabouts.

Before Ole Miss, I earned an **MS in Transportation Engineering at Utah State University** under Dr. Patrick A. Singleton, working on UDOT research in vulnerable road user safety and winter road maintenance. I also worked as a civil engineer in **Kathmandu, Nepal**, on roadway design and traffic studies.

* 🛣️ **NCHRP Project 17-131:** Research team member on a national practitioner guide for Alternative Intersections and Interchanges, led by Dr. Amir M. Molan with HDR and NC State University.
* 🚦 **Intersection Safety:** Applying FHWA's Safe System for Intersections (SSI) method and conflict-point analysis to compare AII design variants.
* ❄️ **Winter Road Maintenance:** Public satisfaction and travel behavior during UDOT winter maintenance (TRB 2025; UDOT Report UT-25.06).
* 📐 **Design & Drafting:** Drawings of alternative intersections in AutoCAD Civil 3D, aligned with FHWA and AASHTO guidance.
